Joint depth is a 2.5D display construction, not measured anatomy. 3D body meshes follow those observed joints: limb thickness, torso depth and head size are a fixed visual template, and body orientation/depth are not measured. Grey translucent limbs appear only with Complete missing limbs and are animation, not observations.
